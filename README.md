How each section is built

Revenue and orders: ₹3.28 Cr from paid orders, across 8,000 orders. That’s about ₹4,531 per paid order, with ₹18.3 L in payments still pending.
Customers: 3,000 in total, of which 973 are active.
Customer health: 522 are at risk and 1,280 are lost.
Open leads: 225 customers signed up but never ordered.
Leads and conversion by platform: your three sales channels (Website, Mobile App, Marketplace) stand in for platforms. A lead is an order placed on that channel, and converted means paid and not cancelled. Conversion is about 90% on each channel.
Monthly revenue and top products: a line chart over the 24 months, and the top 8 products by revenue (hover to see units sold).
Leads to follow up: the signed-up customers with no orders yet, newest first, marked Hot (within 7 days), Follow up (within 30) or Going cold.
Bulk deals pipeline: the top 7% of orders by value from Corporate and Small Business customers. The stages are New lead → Negotiation → Closing → Won → Lost. They map to unordered corporate/small-business sign-ups (valued at an estimate), processing, shipped, delivered and cancelled orders. You can drag cards between stages.
Customer list: “At risk” means the last order was 91–180 days ago, and “Lost” means more than 180 days ago. Each tab shows the top 25 by lifetime value, to keep the page light.
Add lead: a new lead appears in the follow-up list, adds to the platform chart and the open-leads count, and goes into the pipeline’s New lead column if you enter a deal value. Added leads are saved in your browser only. Pipeline drags are not saved and reset on reload.
